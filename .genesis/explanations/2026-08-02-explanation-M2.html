The fan-out is now the default; --agent runs one lane for debugging.

Two decisions worth understanding

1. Dedup can never downgrade a risk

When lanes agree on a location, the highest-confidence finding wins the merge — but the severity is the worst across all of them. If security says CRITICAL at 0.4 confidence and quality says MINOR at 0.95, the merged finding is CRITICAL.

The alternative — take the winner's severity — would let deduplication hide a risk, which inverts the point of having a security lane at all. Tested for order-independence across all 24 permutations of a four-finding collision.

2. The orchestrator does not use ThreadPoolExecutor

It was written with one. A test for the study's orchestration-deadlock failure mode caught it:

AssertionError: orchestrator blocked for 30.0s on a hung lane
assert 30.004 < 5

Three separate reasons the executor cannot provide the needed property:

  • __exit__ calls shutdown(wait=True) — it blocks regardless of what wait(timeout=…) returned, so the deadline is decorative.
  • future.cancel() cannot stop an already-running lane.
  • Since 3.9 its threads are non-daemon and joined at interpreter exit — so a hung lane would also hang the process on the way out, which is fatal in a CI job.

Raw daemon threads give exactly what was required: the review returns on the deadline, and a stuck lane cannot keep the job alive. Verified by the verifier in a subprocess — four lanes sleeping an hour, timeout_s=1.0, total process wall time 1.21s.

What went wrong

B1 — a lane corroborating itself

agreement incremented once per colliding finding, and the merge key was location alone. One specialist reporting two issues on one line produced:

[CRITICAL] app/db.py:10  (sql-injection, security)  [2 lanes agree]

…from the security lane by itself. Two failures at once:

  • Fabrication. A corroboration badge claiming independent confirmation that never happened. On a project whose named primary failure mode is rubber-stamping an almost-right review, this is the single worst field to inflate.
  • Loss. missing-input-validation was deleted outright — category, summary and rationale unrecoverable.

Unreachable through --offline, because the stub breaks after one pattern match per line. That is why no test caught it — and it sits squarely on the shipped Anthropic path.

Fix: merge key is (file_path, line_start, category); agreement counts distinct agent_type values. Two different conclusions about one line are two findings.

B2 — a fixture that proved nothing

The fixture added specifically to demonstrate "duplicate appears once with agreement 2" contained no duplicate: two findings, different lines, both agreement: 1. The test asserted only that locations were unique — which passes with dedupe replaced by the identity function — and its docstring claimed a collision that did not exist.

It looked like proof and was not.

The pattern across M1 and M2

The tests written alongside the code are the least trustworthy artifact produced. Across two milestones, four separate tests asserted something other than what their docstring claimed, and each was found only by mutation — deliberately reintroducing the bug and checking whether anything failed.
